SOURCE TITLE: PTA Complaint Management System User Manual
ORGANIZATION: Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA)
SOURCE URL: https://complaint.pta.gov.pk/Usermanual/User_Manual_CMS_Web.pdf
TOPIC: Stolen Mobile Handset / IMEI Related Complaint

VERIFIED INFORMATION:

The PTA Complaint Management System provides a complaint category for
"Stolen Mobile Handset / IMEI Related Complaint."

The request/nature type can be Blocking or Unblocking.

The complaint may require:
- IMEI Slot 1
- IMEI Slot 2
- IMEI Slot 3
- IMEI Slot 4
- Last Number in Handset for the relevant slot
- Brand

The PTA manual states that complaints regarding stolen mobile handsets
for blocking/unblocking can be registered through the online web portal.

The complaint is routed to the relevant ICT department for blocking or
unblocking of the device.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION:
This source establishes the PTA stolen-handset/IMEI complaint process.
It does not establish police FIR procedures, emergency procedures,
account-security procedures, or any specific recovery timeline.
Those claims must come from separate authoritative sources.
