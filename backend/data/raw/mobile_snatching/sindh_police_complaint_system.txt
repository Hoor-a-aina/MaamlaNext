TOPIC: Police Complaints and FIR Non-Registration

VERIFIED INFORMATION:

Sindh Police operates an IGP Complaint Management System (IGP CMS)
for registering and tracking complaints.

The system covers complaints including:
- Non-registration of FIR
- Police misconduct
- Other police-related complaints

The official Sindh Police IGP CMS guide provides a complaint process
through the system.

The IGP Complaint Management System lists helpline 1715 for complaints.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION:

This source establishes the Sindh Police complaint-management process
and the availability of the IGP CMS.

It does not establish:
- the complete procedure for reporting every type of crime
- emergency response procedures
- phone/IMEI blocking procedures
- investigation or recovery timelines
- specific legal conclusions

Those claims must come from separate authoritative sources.
