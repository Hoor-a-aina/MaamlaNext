SOURCE TITLE: Digital Banking Frauds
ORGANIZATION: State Bank of Pakistan
SOURCE URL: https://www.sbp.org.pk/
TOPIC: Digital Banking Fraud

VERIFIED INFORMATION:

State Bank of Pakistan provides regulatory instructions concerning
digital banking fraud.

Customers who experience suspected digital banking fraud should
contact their bank through the bank's available complaint channels.

Banks have procedures for handling complaints relating to digital
banking fraud.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION:

This source does not establish a universal procedure for every type
of online fraud.

It does not establish criminal investigation procedures, guaranteed
recovery of money, or specific recovery timelines.
