SOURCE TITLE: IGP Complaint Management System Guide
ORGANIZATION: Sindh Police
SOURCE URL: https://igpcms.sindhpolice.gov.pk/assets/guide/IGP_CMS_Guide.pdf
TOPIC: Police Complaint and FIR Non-Registration

VERIFIED INFORMATION:

Sindh Police operates an IGP Complaint Management System (IGP CMS).

The system allows citizens to submit complaints and issues including
non-registration of FIR, poor investigation, police misconduct,
illegal detention or arrest, and other police-related complaints.

The official guide states that complaints can be submitted through
the IGP CMS online portal.

The guide also states that citizens can call or send SMS to helpline
1715 to register and track a complaint.

The guide provides the IGP CMS online portal:
https://igpcms.sindhpolice.gov.pk/

IMPORTANT LIMITATION:

This source establishes a complaint-management mechanism for issues
including FIR non-registration.

It does NOT establish the complete procedure for filing an FIR for
every type of crime.

It does NOT establish investigation timelines, recovery timelines,
criminal penalties, or legal conclusions.
